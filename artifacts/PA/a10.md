# A10: Presentation

## 1. Product presentation

The DreamTask App will will allow any type of user, whether corporate related like startups, consultants, small companies, large businesses, and many others, to have control of all their workflow. The app allow users to have control of all their workflow by controlling and planning their activities and by helping to build the right line-up in order to assemble the perfect project.
The app will allow a user to have their own projects and teams to work with, to search for other users or projects and to view a task of a team that he is in; if the user has the permissions he can add new team members to the team, add more tasks and edit or remove a task that already exists.
